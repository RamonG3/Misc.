How to duel

  1. The ten cards are shuffled and dealt five each. A coin toss decides who calls first.
  2. The caller picks one stat from their top card: Attack, Ranged, Magic, Agility or RuneScape Power.
  3. Both cards are compared on that stat and the higher number wins.
  4. The winner puts both cards at the bottom of their pile and calls the next duel.
  5. Take every card to become RuneScape Duel Champion.

Ties

The printed rules don't cover ties, so this game uses the usual house rule: both cards go into a pot, the same player calls again, and the next winner takes the pot too. In this set only one tie is possible: K'ril Tsutsaroth and Tormented Demon both have 175 Magic.

Your opponent

  • Bronze calls a random stat.
  • Mithril calls the stat on its card that beats the most other cards.
  • Dragon counts cards. It calls the stat most likely to beat whatever you could still be holding.

No opponent ever looks at your card. On a keyboard, keys 1 to 5 call a stat.
